The Loop

Every stage of every Tour de France, 1903–2026, drawn as one route object. Press play and the race writes itself across the map: the early point-to-point giants, the hexagon settling in, the modern foreign starts. Tap or hover any city.

Data & source

Data: LeTourDataSet (Thomas Camminady), one row per stage — men’s 1903–2026, women’s Tour de France Femmes 2022–2025 · route history © ASO · city coordinates geocoded from OpenStreetMap contributors · updated —. Scope & gaps: points are the start/finish cities of each stage, not the ridden roads; no Tour was held during the World Wars (1915–18, 1940–46); the 2026 men’s edition is in progress. A few stage endpoints that couldn’t be geocoded are omitted from that edition’s line.
